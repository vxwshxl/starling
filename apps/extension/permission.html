Let Starling hear you

Speech is recognised on this machine by Whisper and IndicConformer. No audio is uploaded anywhere — it goes to 127.0.0.1:8000 and nowhere else.

Chrome is refusing without asking. To grant it by hand:

  1. Open chrome://settings/content/microphone
  2. Under Allowed to use your microphone, press Add
  3. Paste this extension's address, shown below, and save
